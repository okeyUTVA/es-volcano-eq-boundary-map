Guided Inquiry Worksheet

Objective:

Investigate how spatial patterns of active volcanoes, earthquake epicenters, and tectonic plate boundaries relate to global plate tectonics.

Phase 1: Volcano Distribution

1. Look at the map with Volcanoes enabled.
2. Notice the heavy concentration around the Pacific Ocean perimeter. What famous feature does this form?
3. Click on Mount Fuji, Kilauea, and Eyjafjallajökull. Compare their tectonic settings.

Phase 2: Add Tectonic Boundaries

1. Turn on the Tectonic Plate Layer.
2. How closely do active volcanoes align with red lines (Convergent / Subduction boundaries)?
3. Are there volcanoes in the middle of plates away from boundaries (e.g., Hawaii)? What causes these?

Phase 3: Earthquake Correlation

1. Turn on the Earthquakes Layer.
2. Notice where deep or high-magnitude earthquakes occur. Do they align with subduction zones or transform faults (e.g., San Andreas)?
3. Synthesis: Why do volcanoes and earthquakes occur together in specific geographic belts?

Notes are retained during this active session for easy copying into Canvas assignments.
